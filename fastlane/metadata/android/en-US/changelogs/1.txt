First release.

• Play your computer's audio on the phone over Wi-Fi, in Opus over RTP.
• Call mode: send the phone's microphone back as a capture device on the computer.
• Pair by scanning the sender's QR code, or pick a sender discovered over mDNS.
• Jitter buffer, forward error correction, loss concealment and drift correction,
  with low, balanced and stable latency settings.
• Background playback with headset and notification controls.
• Optional end-to-end encryption of the audio.
